# Demo video script (about 90 seconds)

The shot-by-shot version, with capture settings and DEV's embed rules, is in
[shot-list.md](shot-list.md).

Record on your Mac with a real key, good light on your face and the room quiet.
Screen-record the app, with a small picture-in-picture of your face only for
shot 2. Write a short, real letter to someone (about 4 sentences) with one
moment that actually moves you, and one line that makes you smile.

| # | Time | On screen | Voice-over |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0:00–0:08 | A typed message on a phone, then a scanned handwritten letter with a crossed-out word | "Handwritten letters told you how they felt to write. Typed ones don't." |
| 2 | 0:08–0:18 | Heartwritten consent screen; tick agree; camera on; live heart trace and bpm settle | "Heartwritten reads your heartbeat, breathing and smiles through the webcam, with Presage SmartSpectra. Nothing is saved while you write, and the letter never goes to a server." |
| 3 | 0:18–0:40 | Write the letter. Let the bpm visibly change at the emotional line. Smile once. Pause for a few seconds before the last line. | (Quiet. Let the typing and the live heart carry it.) |
| 4 | 0:40–0:48 | Press **Seal it with my heartbeat**; the wax seal presses in | "You seal it with your own pulse wave. No two seals are the same." |
| 5 | 0:48–1:15 | Open the link in a browser as the recipient. Tap the seal, it cracks. The letter writes itself: the heartline drawing down the margin strip beside each line, standing taller and turning red at the emotional line as the ink warms, the figure at the pen's tip, a pencil stroke under the smiling words, "paused 9 seconds" in the right margin | "They watch it written again, at your pace. That line in the margin is your heartbeat. The ink warms where your heart sped up." |
| 6 | 1:15–1:25 | The postscript at the foot of the letter: "P.S.", with the quickest and steadiest sentences | "And at the end, where your heart was quickest, and where it was steadiest." |
| 7 | 1:25–1:30 | The seal again, with the line "lives only in its link" | "Heartwritten. Letters that carry your heartbeat." |

Tips:

- Keep the camera picture out of shot 5: the letter is the point, and it shows
  the recipient never sees your face.
- If the heart barely moves while writing, read the letter aloud once first, or
  write about something you care about. Don't fake it with exercise; judges
  will see the ink jump.
- Use the 1× replay speed for the emotional part, then skip to the end.
