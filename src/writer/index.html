In your letter
What the camera reads, and where it goes
What it reads
Heart rate, the pulse wave, breathing and smiles, estimated from your face by the Presage SmartSpectra SDK.
What the letter keeps
Numbers only, a few a second. Never video, photos or your face.
Where it goes
Nowhere until you seal it. A sealed letter lives inside its link or file, not on a server. You decide who gets it.
What leaves this computer
The measurement runs here. The SDK contacts Presage only to check the key. Presage says frames and vitals are never in its telemetry, which stays off unless you turn it on below.

Camera estimates, for feeling, not medicine. No diagnosis, no health advice.
