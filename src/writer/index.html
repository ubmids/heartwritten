Heartwritten

Write a letter. Your camera reads your heartbeat, your breathing and your smiles while you write, and the letter keeps them.

  • What the camera reads: heart rate, the pulse wave, breathing and smiles, estimated by the Presage SmartSpectra SDK from your face.
  • What the letter keeps: numbers only, a few a second. Never video, photos or your face.
  • Where it goes: nowhere until you seal it. A sealed letter lives inside its link or file, not on a server. You decide who gets it.
  • What leaves this computer while you write: the measurement runs on this computer. The SDK contacts Presage’s service only to check the key. Presage says frames and vitals are never in its telemetry, which stays off unless you turn it on below.
Your choices
