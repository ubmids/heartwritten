Letters sealed with a heartbeat

Heartwritten

Write a letter while your camera reads your heartbeat. Whoever you send it to watches it being written again, at your pace, every word in the ink your heart gave it.

Read a sample letter

  1. Write in the Heartwritten app on a Mac. The Presage SmartSpectra SDK reads your pulse, breathing and smiles from the camera, on your computer.
  2. Seal it. The letter keeps a few numbers a second, never video, and lives only inside its link or file.
  3. Send the link. It opens here, sending nothing to any server.

The writer is a Mac app. Heart rate and breathing are camera estimates, for feeling, not medicine.
